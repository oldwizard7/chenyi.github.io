Biography

Making agents reliable when the task gets long, scientific, and real.

I am an M.S. student in Data Science at the University of Wisconsin–Madison, where I work with Prof. Xiao Luo. I received my B.S. in Mathematics from Wuhan University.

My research spans LLM agents, agentic reinforcement learning, post-training and evaluation, and AI for Science. I am especially interested in systems that can plan, use tools, learn from interaction, and support their outputs with verifiable evidence.

I have also collaborated with researchers at UCLA's Scalable Analytics Institute and worked on decision-making under uncertainty at HKUST.
